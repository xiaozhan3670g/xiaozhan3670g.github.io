Sudoku Challenge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-08

Sudoku Challenge is an offline Sudoku game.

Information Collection

Sudoku Challenge does not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for contact information, and does not request location access.

Data Sharing

Sudoku Challenge does not share user data with third parties.

Permissions

The app is designed to work offline and does not rely on network access for gameplay.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.
